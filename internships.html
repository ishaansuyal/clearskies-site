Internships

Join the team

Join the team behind ClearSkies as an intern and get hands-on experience with a real, deployed public health project. No experience required, just curiosity and follow-through.

Tracks

Three ways to get involved

We're bringing on interns across three tracks. Pick the one that fits your interests.

Track  ·  Remote-friendly

Research

Data analysis and radon health impact studies. Dig into sensor readings and public health research to help ClearSkies make its case with real numbers.

Track  ·  Remote-friendly

Marketing

Social media, outreach, and grant writing support. Help tell the ClearSkies story and bring in the funding and partnerships that keep units free.

Track  ·  Hands-on

Engineering

Sensor builds, firmware, and deployment logistics. Get hands-on with the hardware and firmware that make continuous radon monitoring possible.

Why Intern With Us

Real ownership, real recognition

Real ownership

Interns get real ownership over their work on a project that's actually deployed and protecting families, not a simulated exercise.

Mentorship

Work directly with a team already recognized with a 2nd place finish at the State Science & Technology Fair of Iowa, a Gloria Barron Prize for Young Heroes nomination, and national news coverage.

Something to point to

Leave with a concrete project to put on college apps, resumes, and LinkedIn, backed by measurable, real-world impact.

Flexible commitment

Remote-friendly for Research and Marketing, hands-on for Engineering. We work around your schedule.
